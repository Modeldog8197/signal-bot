04 / Make the work inspectable

From signal to evidence.

01

Causal features

Wilder RSI (14), MACD (12/26/9), a simple trend average and the preceding 20-session volume mean. A recovery above the declared RSI threshold follows an oversold reading in the previous ten sessions. MACD histogram must be positive.

02

Explicit execution

Signals use the completed daily bar. Orders fill at the next available open. Whole shares, one position per asset, equal maximum allocation, no leverage. RSI ≥ 70, a trend break or the holding limit triggers a next-open exit.

03

Chronological evaluation

The first 75% of sessions are development data; its final 30% form the selection window. The last 25% form the later evaluation period. Indicators may use earlier bars for warmup; portfolio cash resets at each window.

04

Known limitations

Only supplied assets are tested. No survivorship correction, market impact, taxes or liquidity limits. Corporate-action adjustment depends on the data provider. Repeated inspection makes later-period results exploratory; these outputs establish neither significance nor a trading edge.
